GCARC HamFest 2026 - Sunday, October 11

The Gloucester County Amateur Radio Club invites the amateur-radio community to its annual HamFest at the 27-acre Gloucester County 4-H Fairgrounds in Mullica Hill, New Jersey. The event offers outdoor tailgating, covered vendor spaces, forums, license testing, a clubhouse open house, food, and hands-on activities for young people.

Admission and Hours

  • Public gates: 7:00 AM
  • Vendor setup: 6:00 AM
  • Admission: $10 per person
  • Non-ham spouses and children: Free
  • Food: Breakfast and lunch available

Sellers and Testing

  • Tailgate space: $10 per 10-foot outdoor space
  • Covered pavilion space: $15 per 10-foot space
  • ARRL VEC testing: 8:00-11:00 AM
  • Exam fee: $15; advance registration required

Forums, Youth Activities, and Open House

  • 9:00 AM: Doing More with APRS: Application & Hardware - John Zaruba, K2ZA
  • 10:00 AM: ARRL Update - Marty Newingham, AG3I, ARRL Atlantic Division Director
  • 11:00 AM: Space Comm & Youth - Angela Metzger, KE2DRJ
  • 11:00 AM-1:00 PM: GCARC Clubhouse Open House

The Youth Zone continues GCARC's investment in the future of amateur radio with accessible, hands-on electronics and Morse-code activities for children and grandchildren attending with their families.
