📡 2026 Pennsylvania QSO Party

October 10 & 11, 2026 brings one of Pennsylvania amateur radio's signature operating events back to the airwaves. Whether you're an experienced contester, a casual operator, or making your first HF contacts, the Pennsylvania QSO Party is an easy way to get involved, make contacts, and help put the Delaware-Lehigh Amateur Radio Club on the scoreboard.

🗓️ When Is It?

Saturday, October 10: Noon–Midnight EDT
Sunday, October 11: 9:00 AM–6:00 PM EDT

🎙️ Operate From Home or Join W3OK

Have an HF station at home? Fire it up and operate under your own call sign. You don't need to spend the entire weekend in the chair. Every contact helps, whether you make a handful of QSOs or fill the log.

If you're new to amateur radio, don't have an HF station, or simply want to operate alongside other club members, come to the Milk House. The DLARC club station will be participating under the W3OK call sign. Newer operators can get on the air with experienced club members available to help.

🏆 Make Your Home-Station Score Count for DLARC

When setting up your contest logging program, enter Delaware-Lehigh Amateur Radio Club in the Club field. That identifies your entry with DLARC so your individual score can contribute to the club's aggregate score.

Let's see how much DLARC can put on the board this year!

⭐ Find the 2026 Bonus Station: K3ZMC

This year's PA QSO Party bonus station is K3ZMC, operated by the Pottstown Area Amateur Radio Club from Montgomery County as the club celebrates 50 years of organized amateur radio in the Pottstown area. K3ZMC plans three networked stations, with activity focused on CW and SSB across the available contest bands.

Here's why you'll want to find them: each valid QSO with K3ZMC is worth 200 bonus points. Listen for them throughout both operating periods and look for opportunities on additional bands and modes.

💻 Logging the Party

Contest logging software makes the weekend much easier. The PA QSO Party lists several programs that support the event, including N1MM Logger+, N3FJP, and WriteLog. Use the latest version of whichever program you choose and set it up before the contest begins.

After the Party, logs are submitted electronically in Cabrillo 3.0 format. Even a small log is worth submitting, and PAQP strongly encourages everyone who participates to send theirs in.

👋 No HF Station? No Problem.

Come to the Milk House and operate W3OK. This is a great opportunity to try HF contest operating, learn the rhythm of search-and-pounce or calling CQ, and get some on-air time with other DLARC members.

📣 Let's Get DLARC on the Air!

Ten contacts or a thousand, they all start the same way: turn on the radio and join the Party. Operate from home, join the crew at W3OK, submit your log, and make sure your home-station entry identifies Delaware-Lehigh Amateur Radio Club.

Full rules, FAQs, operating aids, county information, logging resources, spotting information, and contest updates are available from the official Pennsylvania QSO Party website .
