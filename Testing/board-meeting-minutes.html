📝 Minutes of the September 2026 Board Meeting

Date: September 21, 2026
Time: 7:00 PM

President's Report

President Dean Guth, AB3BD, reported that there were no items requiring discussion during the President's Report.

Secretary's Report

The Board approved the August Board Meeting minutes as published in the club newsletter.

Treasurer's Report

The Treasurer's Report was submitted electronically prior to the meeting and was accepted by the Board.

Board Business

  • Hamfest Planning: Planning continues to move forward for the joint hamfest project. The committee reported productive discussions with Schnecksville Fire Company regarding the proposed venue. The enclosed pavilion, ample parking, and tailgating opportunities make the location a strong candidate for the event. Approximately thirty vendors have already expressed interest, and the committee has begun the process of securing ARRL sponsorship. Organizers are also exploring the creation of a 501(c)(3) organization to provide legal protection for the participating organizations. A joint meeting between the participating organizations is being planned for November, and preliminary promotional materials have been developed. The Board expressed appreciation for the significant progress being made toward organizing the event.
  • Club Mailing Address: The Board approved changing the club's official mailing address to the Northampton County 911 Center. Club records, organizational registrations, and other materials referencing the previous mailing address will be updated in a coordinated effort led by Treasurer Larry Butler, KC3JTK.
  • Marathon Communications Support: The Board was informed that W3OI will contact the club regarding possible volunteer communications support for the upcoming marathon scheduled for November 15.

Old Business

  • Surplus Club Equipment: Work continues on preparing surplus club equipment for sale to members. Photographs and equipment descriptions are being finalized before the items are listed.
  • Club Equipment: The Board continued discussions regarding replacement grills for club activities. Various options were reviewed, including propane and charcoal grills. The topic was tabled pending further evaluation.

Adjournment

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 7:19 PM.

Wayne Westfield, N3FQ, Secretary
