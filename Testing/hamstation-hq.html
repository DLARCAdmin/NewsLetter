🖥️ HamStation HQ

Ken Kalous (KE5VXX) introduced his new project on the McKinney Amateur Radio Club weekly repeater net and reached out to share it with the club. Shared here in the W3OK Corral as being of general interest for our members.

HamStation is an all-in-one amateur radio application for Windows, Linux, and macOS, designed to bring many of the tools we use every day into a single, modern program. It includes logging, satellite tracking with Doppler correction, interactive maps, solar and propagation information, APRS support, equipment inventory, QSL card design, Morse code practice, and much more.

Ken is actively developing HamStation and adding new features based on feedback from the amateur radio community. The app is fully functional and free forever; an optional paid upgrade removes the nag screens.

Best of all, Ken is offering a free lifetime license to everyone at MARC. Other club's members can download and try it out. To learn more, follow the project, or try future beta releases, visit HamStationHQ.com.

- Ken Kalous, KE5VXX
